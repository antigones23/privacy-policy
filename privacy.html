Around Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Around is a neighborhood community app that allows users to create accounts, request or offer help, publish community posts, give away items, and interact with other users. This Privacy Policy explains what information Around collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Before publishing this page, replace YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com with the email address you want users and Apple to contact.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Around, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, login credentials, and user identifier.
  • Profile information: profile photo and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • User content: help requests, offers, bulletin-board posts, comments, replies, item listings, photos, and other content you submit.
  • Neighborhood or location information: an address, neighborhood, or location that you enter or attach to a post or request.
  • Technical information: device and browser type, IP address, app version, diagnostic logs, crash information, and interaction data generated when you use the app.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us for assistance, reporting content, or raising a safety concern.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display requests, posts, listings, comments, profiles, and responses to other users.
  • Provide neighborhood-based app features.
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve Around.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reports of abuse, fraud, or unsafe conduct.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies.

Information Visible to Other Users

Information that you publish through Around may be visible to other users. This can include your name, profile photo, posts, comments, item listings, requests, replies, and any location details you choose to include. Do not post sensitive personal information that you do not want other users to see.

Service Providers and Disclosure

Around uses third-party service providers to host, store, secure, maintain, and deliver the app. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services for Around. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the public, to investigate misuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the app.

Advertising and Tracking

Around does not sell personal information and does not use personal information to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies for targeted advertising.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the app, maintain user accounts, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal requirements, and protect the safety and integrity of the service. Some information may remain temporarily in backups after deletion.

Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may update certain account information through the app. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal information by using the account-deletion option in the app or by emailing YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com. We may retain limited information when required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Around is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can review and delete it where required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy is updated.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com.
